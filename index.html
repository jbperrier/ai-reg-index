The law moves. The citation should move with it.

A register of every statute, regulation, supervisory letter and standard that sets AI governance and security obligations on an enterprise. Each entry is written in plain language, kept to one schema, cross‑mapped to the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001, and cited to the primary source.

A monitor re‑reads every source on a schedule. When the text changes, the entry changes, and the change is recorded below with a date.

Every entry states the day it was last read against the source.

A regulatory reference that is quietly out of date is worse than none. So the register is machine‑monitored: a scheduled job re‑reads each primary source, compares it to the last copy on record, and raises the difference for editorial review.

Nothing is published from a secondary source. Trackers and law‑firm commentary tell us to go and look. The text we publish comes from the gazette, the register, or the issuing authority.

Sources re‑read   daily during session, weekly otherwise
Editorial review   before any entry changes
Corrections   recorded with a date, never silently
